## The Problem

Traditional online exams suffer from:
- ❌ **Lack of supervision** → Students can cheat easily
- ❌ **No accountability** → Parents don't trust results
- ❌ **Poor discipline** → Students lack focus
- ❌ **Limited evidence** → Disputes cannot be resolved
- ❌ **Low perceived value** → Cannot justify premium pricing

**Result:** Online courses are seen as lower quality than in-person programs.

---

## Our Solution

We transform remote exams into **professional, exam-center-quality experiences** through comprehensive digital monitoring technology.

### Core Features

#### 🎥 **Dual-Camera Monitoring System**
- **Laptop camera**: Monitors student face and attention
- **Phone camera**: 360° workspace surveillance via mobile device
- **Real-time streaming** to proctors with automatic snapshots
- **Prevents cheating** better than physical exam halls

#### 🎤 **Live Audio Communication**
- **Two-way proctor communication** for immediate intervention
- **Student call requests** for assistance
- **Push-to-talk controls** for efficient supervision
- **Complete audio logging** for compliance

#### 🔒 **Advanced Anti-Cheating Security**
- **Fullscreen lockdown** with automatic violation detection
- **Tab/window switching prevention** and logging
- **Keyboard controls disabled** (copy/paste, dev tools, right-click)
- **Multiple monitor detection**
- **Every violation logged** with timestamp and duration

#### 👨‍💼 **Real-Time Proctor Dashboard**
- Monitor **15-20 students simultaneously** (vs. 10 in physical centers)
- **Live video feeds** from all cameras
- **One-click interventions** (warnings, camera checks, disqualification)
- **Violation alerts** with severity scoring
- **Complete session recordings** for review

#### 📊 **Comprehensive Analytics**
- **Every action timestamped**: answers, navigation, violations
- **Behavior pattern detection**: suspicious activity scoring
- **Detailed session reports**: complete audit trail per student
- **Performance metrics**: time management, question difficulty
- **Export capabilities**: PDF, CSV, JSON

#### 🔐 **Secure Access Control**
- **Unique exam codes** (time-limited, one-time use)
- **Multi-factor verification**: account + code + identity check
- **Pre-exam setup validation**: cameras, workspace, consent
- **Session management**: automatic submission, timing controls
